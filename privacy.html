Privacy Policy

How Lansing Mechanical LLC handles the details you share with us. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

When you use the request form on this website, we receive the details you type in: your name, phone number, email address if you give one, your zip code, what you need help with and how soon. When you call or text us, we see your phone number.

How we use it

Only to get back to you, book and carry out the work you asked about, and follow up on that job. We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

How the form works

The request form opens your own email app with your details filled in, addressed to us at lansing@lmtxmail.com. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Other services on this site

The map on our contact page is provided by Google Maps, and the website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google may receive basic technical information from your browser when those load. Our financing link takes you to Wells Fargo's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

To ask what details we hold about you, or to have them deleted, call or text (940) 229-0715 or email lansing@lmtxmail.com.
